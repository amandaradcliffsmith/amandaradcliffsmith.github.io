The problem

Sound familiar?

They ace the worksheet. Then the test changes one number, and they're lost.

“Just tell me the steps.” You hear it every single day.

They can flip and multiply. Ask why it works, and the room goes quiet.

{% include note.html text="that's math mimicking" %}

It's a habit, not a failing

Two sides of the same habit

Most of us learned math by watching someone show the steps, then copying them. So that's how we teach: I do, we do, you do.

Students get very good at following along. The understanding never gets built. That's math mimicking: an imitation-based teaching habit on one side, and students who can reproduce procedures without knowing why they work on the other.

None of this is a teacher problem. It's a habit almost all of us inherited, and habits can change.
