Ways to work together

Start where your teachers are

Workshops

Hands-on professional learning for math teams. Teachers experience sense-making as learners, then leave with routines they can use the next morning.

Good for: PD days, math departments, grade-level teams

Coaching

Ongoing support for individual teachers, teams, and math coaches, in planning and in the classroom, so new habits actually stick.

Good for: teachers and coaches ready to go deeper

School and district partnerships

Multi-session work that builds sense-making across grade levels and buildings, designed around your goals and your curriculum.

Good for: schools and districts making a lasting shift
